# Role presets

Starting suggestions for the setup interview. They are suggestions only: the person's answers always win. Each preset lists people to never miss (`must_see`), things to capture (`trackers`), what to ignore, and suggested Notion columns in case they need a new database.

## VC / investor at a fund

- **Must see:** Colleagues (fund domain); Portfolio founders (optional); LPs (optional).
- **Trackers:**
  - **Deals** (key: company)
    - counts: founder pitches; intros or co-invest invitations from other investors and angels; companies forwarded by colleagues
    - not: updates from existing portfolio companies; other funds pitching for an LP commitment; vendors selling to the fund; event invites; job applications
    - extract: website, founders, round, round size, geography, sector
- **Ignore:** newsletters, event marketing, recruiters.
- **Columns:** Company (title), Stage (Intake, Screening, Due Diligence, IC, Invested, Declined), Owner, Source, Website, Round, Round Size, Geography, Sector, Summary.

## Angel or scout

- **Must see:** Fund partners they scout for, or their syndicate lead; co-angels.
- **Trackers:**
  - **Deals** (key: company): same as VC. Add "syndicate invitations from platforms" if they want them.
- **Ignore:** newsletters, mass platform digests (unless they opt in).
- **Columns:** same as VC; usually Notion only, no CRM.

## Founder

- **Must see:** Co-founders & team (company domain); Investors & board; Key customers.
- **Trackers:**
  - **Investor conversations** (key: firm or person)
    - counts: investors reaching out; intros to investors; replies from investors you pitched; term sheets and data-room requests
    - not: newsletters from funds; generic "office hours" mailings; investment-platform spam
    - extract: firm, investor name, stage they invest in, next step
  - **Customer & sales leads** (key: company)
    - counts: inbound demo or pricing requests; customer intros; RFPs
    - not: vendors selling to you; cold outreach from agencies
    - extract: company, contact, use case, size or budget if stated
  - **Requests & deadlines** (key: subject): optional. Anything from a must-see person with a date or a clear ask.
- **Ignore:** vendor cold outreach, recruiters, event marketing.
- **Columns (investors):** Name (title), Firm, Stage (Contacted, Intro call, Diligence, Term sheet, Passed), Source, Next step, Summary.

## Sales / BD / partnerships

- **Must see:** Manager & team; Key accounts (customer domains); Partners.
- **Trackers:**
  - **New leads** (key: company)
    - counts: inbound requests; referrals; replies to outreach showing interest; event follow-ups with a concrete ask
    - not: existing customers' routine support; vendors selling to you; auto-replies
    - extract: company, contact, title, need, budget or timeline if stated
  - **Partnership requests** (key: company)
    - counts: companies proposing integrations, co-marketing, reselling
    - not: generic "let's connect" with no proposal
  - **Account risk** (key: company): optional. Must-see account emails mentioning cancel, churn, unhappy, competitor, renewal.
- **Ignore:** internal newsletters, tool notifications.
- **Columns:** Company (title), Stage (New, Qualified, Meeting, Proposal, Won, Lost), Owner, Contact, Source, Value, Summary.

## Operator or exec

- **Must see:** Boss or leadership; direct team (company domain); key clients or stakeholders.
- **Trackers:**
  - **Requests & deadlines** (key: subject)
    - counts: a must-see person asks you for something, or a date or deadline is mentioned
    - not: FYI updates, automated reports
    - extract: who, what, due date
  - **Approvals** (key: subject): optional. Anything waiting for your sign-off.
- **Ignore:** company-wide announcements, tool notifications.
- **Columns:** Task (title), Status (To do, Waiting, Done), From, Due, Source, Summary.

## Something else

Build from scratch: ask what their job is, who they can't afford to miss, and what a great incoming email looks like. Usually one or two trackers.
